Legacy & Origin

Saranathan College of Engineering was founded in 1998 by the Saranathan Educational Trust. It was named in revered memory of the visionary educationist, Prof. V. Saranathan, the legendary Principal of National College, Tiruchirappalli.

The institution was built to bring world-class technical education to rural and urban youth alike in central Tamil Nadu, fostering discipline, character, and cutting-edge technical expertise.

Affiliations & Accreditation

  • Affiliated to Anna University, Chennai
  • Approved by AICTE, New Delhi
  • Recognized under Section 2(f) and 12(B) of UGC Act
  • Accredited by NAAC & NBA for core engineering programs

Vision & Mission

Institutional Vision:

To evolve into a premier technical institution of global repute, imparting quality engineering education, driving innovation, and moulding responsible global citizens.

Institutional Mission:

To provide student-centric Outcome Based Education (OBE), establish state-of-the-art research laboratories, and nurture industry collaborations.

Placements & Training Cell

Our dedicated Training and Placement Cell conducts intense training programs starting from year one, covering quantitative aptitude, competitive programming, soft skills, and mock technical interviews. Top recruiters include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, Zoho, HCL, and Texas Instruments.

Research & Innovation Cell

The R&D Cell encourages faculty and student research projects funded by national agencies including DST, AICTE, TNSCST, and ISRO. The campus features specialized research labs for 3D Modeling, AI Computing, Embedded Systems, and Mechatronics.

Campus & Hostel Infrastructure

Located along Tiruchirappalli - Madurai Highway (NH 45B) at Panjappur, the 40-acre campus houses separate hostels for men and women (Kaveri & Pennar blocks), a modern sports complex, high-speed 1 Gbps Wi-Fi, and a fleet of buses servicing Tiruchirappalli and surrounding districts.
